Our Story

Wabi-Sabi Beauty 侘び寂びの美しさ

花の美

Where cherry blossoms
meet eternal love

Inspired by the Japanese philosophy of mono no aware — the bittersweet beauty of fleeting moments — we capture your wedding day with the sensitivity and artistry it deserves.

Each session is choreographed like a carefully composed haiku: nothing excessive, everything intentional. We find poetry in a glance, a petal's fall, the soft light of a golden hour.

With over a decade of experience photographing ceremonies across Japan and beyond, we bring a refined aesthetic eye to every frame.
